WiggleWars

Turn-based worm warfare · 2–4 players · peer-to-peer

How to play
  • Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D walk · W/Enter jump · ↑/↓ aim · hold & release Space to fire · 1–5 weapons · Tab switch worm · drag or screen edges to pan · wheel zooms · C recenters
  • Touch: drag back from your worm like a slingshot and let go to fire · buttons walk, jump, pick weapons and fire · drag to pan, pinch to zoom · tap one of your worms to switch at the start of a turn
  • Fire ends your turn after a 3 s retreat. Water and map edges are deadly. Last team standing wins.
